The research loop

  1. Edit the ICP here and Save.
  2. Create a research request and paste its brief into a Codex task.
  3. The agent researches, self-checks with crm validate, and deposits into inbox/.
  4. The Import page tracks delivered-vs-requested, dedupes, and explains rejects.
  5. Queue the good ones and start dialing from Today.
